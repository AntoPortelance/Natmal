# 낱말 찾기 (Natmal) – Learn Korean with word search

**Play now:** https://antoportelance.github.io/Natmal/

Natmal is a Korean word search game designed for your phone. Each cell of the grid holds one Hangul syllable block (like 한), and you find hidden Korean words from their meaning. The game takes you from your very first words all the way to advanced vocabulary, in five levels named after taekwondo belts.

It installs on your home screen like a regular app, works offline, and costs nothing: no App Store, no account, no ads.

## Features

- **5 belts, 20 themes, 320 words.** From family and food at the white belt to four-character idioms (사자성어) at the black belt.
- **One syllable per cell**, the natural way to read Korean.
- **Two modes per theme:** Discovery (Korean and translation shown) and Challenge (translation only).
- **Belt exams** that test every word of a level before you move on.
- **Native pronunciation** of every word you find, using your phone's Korean voice.
- **French and English** interface and translations, switchable in Settings.
- **Works offline** after the first launch.
- **Progress saved on your device**, with a word counter for every theme.

## How to play

1. Pick a theme on the home screen.
2. Find the Korean words hidden in the grid. You can either:
   - **swipe** your finger across the syllables, or
   - **tap the first syllable, then the last one.**
3. When a word is found, it's highlighted, spoken aloud, and crossed off the list. Tap any found word to hear it again.
4. Stuck? Tap **Hint**: the first syllable of a hidden word flashes.

Words hide in more directions as you progress:

| Belt | Grid | Directions |
|---|---|---|
| White belt (흰 띠) | 7 × 7 | Left to right, top to bottom |
| Yellow belt (노란 띠) | 7 × 7 | + diagonal going down |
| Green belt (초록 띠) | 8 × 8 | + diagonal going up |
| Blue belt (파란 띠) | 8 × 8 | + backward words |
| Black belt (검은 띠) | 9 × 9 | All 8 directions |

When a grid is finished, a results panel slides up. Tap the grid above it (or **See the grid**) to slide it back down and look at your finished grid, then tap **See results** to bring it back.

## Progression rules

### Themes

Each theme has 16 words, and each game uses 8 of them.

- **Discovery mode**
  - Your first game in a theme gives you 8 new words.
  - After that, each game mixes **at most 2 new words** with 6 words you've already found. New words carry a small red dot.
  - The theme counter (for example `10/16`) shows how many words you've discovered. It never goes down.
  - **First star ⭐:** discover all 16 words (5 games).
- **Challenge mode**
  - Locked 🔒 until all 16 words are discovered.
  - Only the translation is shown: you have to recall the Korean yourself.
  - **Second star ⭐:** complete one Challenge grid.

### Belt exams

Every belt ends with an exam that covers its 64 words (4 themes × 16).

- Each test draws **16 random words** from the ones you haven't validated yet.
- Words are validated **only when you complete the test**. If you leave early, nothing is validated and the next test is a fresh draw.
- 8 × 8 grid, words read left to right or top to bottom, translation only.
- 3 hints per test.
- Once **all 64 words are validated** (4 successful tests), the next belt unlocks.

You don't have to finish the themes first. If you already know some Korean, you can go straight to the exam, which works as a placement test.

## Install the app on your phone

Natmal is a web app (a PWA). Instead of downloading it from a store, you add it to your home screen from your browser. It then gets its own icon, opens full screen without the browser bar, and works offline.

> **Important:** install it from the address above (`antoportelance.github.io/Natmal/`), not from a copy or a link inside another app, or your progress won't be kept in the right place.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open **Safari** (the blue compass icon).
   - Safari is the most reliable choice. On recent iOS versions, other browsers can add apps to the home screen too, but if you don't see the option, use Safari.
   - If you opened the link from a messaging app (WhatsApp, Messenger, Slack…), it may be showing inside that app. Look for **Open in Safari** or a compass icon, usually in a menu at the top or bottom.
2. Go to **https://antoportelance.github.io/Natmal/** and wait for the home screen of the game to appear.
3. Tap the **Share** button: a square with an arrow pointing up.
   - On iPhone, it's in the bar at the bottom of the screen. If you don't see the bar, scroll up slightly or tap the bottom of the screen to bring it back.
   - On some iOS versions, it's behind the **⋯** button next to the address bar.
   - On iPad, it's at the top right.
4. In the menu that opens, scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen** (French: « Sur l'écran d'accueil »).
   - Not in the list? Scroll to the very bottom, tap **Edit Actions…**, and add it.
5. Leave the name as **낱말 찾기** (or rename it), make sure **Open as Web App** is on if you see that option, then tap **Add** at the top right.
6. The app icon (a green grid with a red stroke) appears on your home screen. Launch the game from this icon from now on.

### Android

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to **https://antoportelance.github.io/Natmal/**.
3. Either:
   - tap the **Install** banner or pop-up if Chrome offers one, or
   - tap the **⋮** menu at the top right, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen** on some phones).
4. Confirm with **Install** (or **Add**).
5. The icon appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.

Other browsers (Samsung Internet, Edge, Firefox) have a similar option in their menu, usually called **Add page to** → **Home screen** or **Install**.

### After installing

- **Open it once with an internet connection.** On the first launch, the phone downloads and saves the game. After that, it works in airplane mode, on the subway, anywhere.
- **Keep the icon on your home screen.** Removing it deletes your progress (see below).
- **Updates are automatic.** When the game is updated, the new version loads the next time you open the app with a connection. There's nothing to reinstall, and your progress is kept.
- On iPhone, the app may not appear in the App Library (the last home screen page). Find it on your home screen or with Spotlight search.

## Get a better Korean voice

The pronunciation uses the voices installed on your phone. The default one is often basic, and a better one takes two minutes to install and works offline.

**iPhone:** Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Korean → download **Yuna (Enhanced)** or **Yuna (Premium)**.
Depending on your iOS version, web apps may not always use the Premium voices, but it's worth trying.

**Android:** Settings → search for **Text-to-speech** → Preferred engine settings → Install voice data → **Korean**.

You can turn all sounds on or off in the game's **⚙️ Settings**. On iPhone, the found-word "ding" follows the silent switch.

## Your progress

Your progress (discovered words, stars, validated exam words, language and sound settings) is saved **only on your phone**. There's no account and nothing is sent anywhere.

- It's kept as long as the app stays on your home screen. The game asks the phone for persistent storage, so the system won't clear it on its own, even when storage runs low.
- It's **erased** if you:
  - delete the app icon from your home screen (iPhone),
  - uninstall the app or clear Chrome's data (Android),
  - clear your browser's website data.
- It isn't shared between devices: your phone and your computer each keep their own progress.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| I can't find **Add to Home Screen** | Make sure you're in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), not inside another app's browser. On iPhone, scroll to the bottom of the Share menu and use **Edit Actions…** |
| The game doesn't work offline | Open the app once with a connection, wait a few seconds, close it, and try again. |
| No sound | Check the ⚙️ Settings sound option and your phone's volume. On iPhone, the "ding" is muted by the silent switch. |
| The voice doesn't speak | Install a Korean voice (see above), then restart the app. |
| I don't see the latest version | Open the app with a connection, close it completely (swipe it away in the app switcher), and reopen it. |
| My progress is gone | It's stored on the device only, so it can't be recovered after deleting the app or clearing browser data. |

## For developers

The whole game is a static site with no build step and no dependencies:

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The entire game: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the word lists |
| `sw.js` | Service worker for offline play and automatic updates |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | App name, icons, and colors for home screen installation |
| `icon-180.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` | App icons |
| `.nojekyll` | Tells GitHub Pages to serve the files as they are |

**Hosting:** GitHub Pages, from the `main` branch, root folder. Any static host with HTTPS works.

**Publishing an update:**
1. Replace the changed files in the repository.
2. In `sw.js`, bump the cache version (for example `natmal-v12` → `natmal-v13`) so every phone picks up the new version.
3. Commit. GitHub Pages redeploys automatically in about a minute (check the **Actions** tab).

**Adding words:** each theme in `index.html` lists its words as `korean=french=english`, separated by `;`. Keep 16 words per theme, at least 2 syllables per word, and avoid a word hidden inside another word of the same theme.

Progress is stored in the browser's `localStorage` under the key `natmal-progress-v1`.
